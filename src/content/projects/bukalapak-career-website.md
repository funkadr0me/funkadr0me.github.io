## Hiring for the next stage

Bukalapak was preparing to go public, and growing meant hiring. The old careers page was out of date and no longer looked like the company Bukalapak wanted to be. The new one had to win over people across Indonesia, and in other tech hubs such as Singapore and Australia.

## Working out what it should say

I sat down with Human Resources and the C-level to understand what they wanted the site to do and what it had to include. With the UX designers, I worked on the big concept, the flow of the site and its information architecture.

## The words

I wrote the homepage copy in the same tone and voice as the product, so candidates heard the company they would be joining.

## What changed

Compared with the old page, visits tripled, and so did the time people spent browsing.

The site has since been retired. Before that, in November 2021, it moved to a simpler look that kept this version’s big concept.

## My part
