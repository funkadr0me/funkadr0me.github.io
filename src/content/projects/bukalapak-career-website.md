## Hope, happiness and millions of dreams

The old careers page opened with a big promise. Within each of our millions of transactions, it said, there was "hope and happiness that transcends millions of dreams." It was sincere, and very much a careers page.

By early 2021, Bukalapak was heading for its IPO and hiring fast: across Indonesia, and for new offices in Australia and Singapore. The careers site was the first thing a candidate would check, and ours still looked like the startup of home offices and shared spaces. The new one had to show a solid tech company. The CEO's office ran the project (some of the notes came from the CEO himself), and I led the words.

## No big words this time

Careers sites tend to be proud of themselves: big words, jargon, a mission in every line. We went the other way, with two principles from the voice we'd built for the product: use plain words, and be a good friend. Simple, direct reasons to join, and nothing else.

The values show the difference best. The old page said: "We believe that data is king. We drive our strategic decisions by analyzing trillions of data points from hundreds of experiments." The new value, worded by management, was "Jangan baper, lihat data!" (Don't take it personally, look at the data!) The line I wrote for it was shorter still: "You let data be the impartial judge."

The values came from management word for word, and Bukalapak was proud of their Indonesian roots, so they kept their own words. My job was the copy around them: what each one meant, so talent from abroad got the gist.

## A quiz they'd actually play

Bukalapak was known as a fun place to work, and the people we wanted most (young, early in their careers) were sharing BuzzFeed personality quizzes. So we made one. The designers, the illustrators, the brand team and I worked it out day by day, and we played more BuzzFeed quizzes than ever (research, of course).

Each question turned a value into an ordinary situation: "There's a big meeting in 15 minutes, but you haven't got your lunch. What you gonna do?" Or simply: "What's your spirit animal?" While the result loaded, the site admitted what it was doing: "Calculating our best guess..."

The result was made to share: "Wohooo... Just found out which Bukalapak value matches with me! Find out yours at careers.bukalapak.com!"

## A simpler look, the same idea

In November 2021, a few months after its IPO, the company regrouped as BUKA, a group of tech companies with Bukalapak as one of them, and its careers page moved to a simpler version. The look changed, and most of the big concept stayed.

Millions of dreams had made way for one small question: "Which Bukalapak value suits you the most?"
