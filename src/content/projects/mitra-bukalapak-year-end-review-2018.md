## Shopkeepers who like to be seen

Mitra Bukalapak is Bukalapak’s offline-to-online business: a close-knit community of mom-and-pop shops. The owners respect each other, and they also like to show off what they’ve achieved. Bukalapak wanted to thank them for a year of loyalty and keep them transacting on the platform.

## A syukuran in the neighbourhood

Every Mitra who qualified got a recap of their year: their transactions, dotted with small facts and trivia. To make it a thank-you rather than a statement of figures, we told it as a story, and opened it with one question: “Eh, udah tahun baru aja nih! Pengen tau warungmu udah ngehasilin apa aja, setahun kemarin?” (The new year’s already here! Want to see what your warung earned last year?)

In the Mitra’s own neighbourhood, a syukuran, a thanksgiving gathering, is under way. Pak RT, the head of the neighbourhood, is there to honour his most outstanding residents. Each Mitra received an award that matched what they had achieved, and every recap could be shared on social media, so they could show it off.

## Numbers worth showing

I worked with the data scientists to choose numbers we could pull reliably and that were worth showing. Then I developed the concept and flow with the UX designers, and wrote the copy, down to the name of every award. A Mitra who sold plane, train and bus tickets became a Pendekar Mata Angin, a warrior of the compass.

## What changed

In the first week, Mitra shared their results widely with each other, and some posted them on social media.
