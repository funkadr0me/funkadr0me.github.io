## Bragging rights

A Mitra who sells an electricity token at midnight earns a reputation. (Some never seem to close, like the warung Madura in the joke: the corner shop that, when the world ends, will only close for half a day.) Mitras, the small shops that sell through Mitra Bukalapak, love to brag: their biggest sale, their latest one, their shop's new look.

The product team wanted more of them opening the app every day, with more transactions to follow. They also wanted it funny, because that's what Mitras liked. At the end of 2018, we gave every Mitra a look back at their year. I planned the story and its theme, worked out the interaction with the designers, and wrote the copy.

## A syukuran in the neighborhood

A syukuran is a thanksgiving gathering. In the neighborhoods where Mitras live, the head of the neighborhood (the Ketua RT) usually leads it, so he became our host. Each recap opened with his welcome speech: "Selamat datang di syukuran Warung Budi" (Welcome to Warung Budi's syukuran).

The Community team spent their days in the Mitras' WhatsApp groups, Facebook pages and meetups. They passed on the Mitras' phrases and banter, and I wrote the Ketua RT in that voice.

## Big words for small numbers

Every screen with a number was the hard part. Many Mitras had small figures, and the words still had to sound like bragging rights. So the Ketua RT never just reports. He cups his ear first: "Saya dengar laporan, nih!" (I've heard the report!)

  <img src="/images/work/mitra-year-end-review-2018/report.jpg" alt="The Ketua RT cupping his ear behind a report, over a turnover and transaction count" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The year's turnover and transactions, as reported to the Ketua RT.</figcaption>
</figure>

The data scientists gave me the raw numbers: when Mitras sold, how much, and what they restocked. I turned them into small scenes around the warung. When a Mitra's customers mostly bought in the afternoon, we knew when, but not who (Mitras don't label their customers). So the Ketua RT guesses, and admits it: "Ketebak nih… yang beli, adek-adek pulang sekolah!" (Called it… it's the kids on their way home from school!)

The pictures did half the bragging. With the UI illustrator, I worked out how the Ketua RT reacts to each number and how the sky around the warung sets the mood: a sunset for the afternoon buyers, a bright morning for the restocking.

Numbers we couldn't pull reliably, or that had nothing to say, stayed out.

## Pendekar, Jawara, Lentera

Every recap ended with a title. I borrowed the big words of government honors and formal salutations, and handed them to warungs. There were five or six. A Mitra who sold plane, train and bus tickets became a Pendekar Mata Angin (Warrior of the Compass). The one with the most bill payments became a Jawara Beresin Tagihan (Bill-Settling Champion). My favorite went to the Mitras whose electricity tokens topped everything else: Lentera Warga, the neighborhood's lantern.

## Nothing to count, still something to celebrate

Some Mitras had no data yet, and they still got a syukuran. "Hasil Warungmu Belum Bisa Diitung" (Your warung's results can't be counted yet), the screen said. "Meski begitu, hasilnya patut disyukuri, kok." (Even so, they're worth being thankful for.) The button answered the way a resident answers the Ketua RT: "Siap Laksanakan!" (Ready to carry it out!)

## Back where the bragging started

The cards went where Mitras brag best: their WhatsApp groups and their Facebook walls. The Community team sent me the screenshots (note to self: save the screenshots).

The Mitras who used to brag about a midnight sale now had a title to brag with.
