## Two voices, one brand

In early 2018, Bukalapak spoke two ways. Its ads were witty and down to earth. Its app was stiff and functional, and often didn’t sound like the people using it. Nobody had decided how the product should speak.

## Listening first

We started by asking. We interviewed dozens of stakeholders, from design leads to the VP of product, about the words in the product and what they wanted from them. We also went back through earlier user research on how people heard our messages.

## One day, two kinds of writers

Then we put everything on the table in a one-day workshop. In the room were the product content strategists, who wrote the app, and the creative content strategists, who wrote the ads. With both there, the product voice could borrow what the brand had already earned.

By the end of the day we had a persona and five principles:

- **User-centric.** Tell people only what matters most, and make them the subject of the action.
- **Be a good friend.** Every line is a thoughtful suggestion that helps them decide quickly.
- **Offer solutions.** Every word guides people toward their goal.
- **Use plain words.** Common words with clear meanings, and no new jargon unless we’re teaching it.
- **Be consistent.** Everything comes from one big idea.

Over the following months, the team turned these into writing guidelines and tips, each tied back to a principle. One of them sets the tone by the user’s situation: joyful when things go well, casual when they’re neutral, serious and straight when they go wrong, with an apology where one is owed.

## Into the design system

A voice only works if people reach for it while they design. I brought the tone and voice into Bazaar, Bukalapak’s design system, so the guidelines sat next to the components that carried the words.

## What changed

The product started to sound like the brand people already knew: more colloquial, closer to how its users talk. As Bukalapak grew beyond its marketplace into new products, the same voice stretched to fit them, and the principles are still published in Bukalapak’s brand guideline.
